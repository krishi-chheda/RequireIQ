# Engagement Brief: Meridian Bank Customer Onboarding Platform

Prepared by Northgate Advisory for Meridian Bank plc. Distribution: steering
committee only.

## 1. Background

Meridian Bank currently onboards personal current account customers through a
branch-first process supported by a 2014-era origination system. Average time to
account activation is 6.5 working days. Digital abandonment on the existing web
form runs at 61%.

Meridian has appointed Northgate Advisory to run a 10-week discovery and to
deliver the target-state customer onboarding platform.

## 2. Scope baseline

The following sits inside the agreed scope baseline as of the date of this
brief. Anything added after the baseline date of 24 July 2026 must pass through
the change control process defined in section 7 of the master services
agreement.

- Personal current account onboarding for UK-resident retail customers.
- Identity verification, sanctions screening and politically exposed person
  screening.
- Application status tracking for the customer and for the branch network.
- Handoff of activated accounts into the existing core banking platform.

## 3. Out of scope

- Business and commercial account onboarding.
- Mortgage and secured lending origination.
- Replacement of the core banking platform itself.

## 4. Success criteria

The programme must reduce median time to account activation from 6.5 working
days to under 1 working day. The programme should reduce digital abandonment on
the onboarding journey below 30%.

## 5. Delivery approach

Discovery runs from 8 June 2026 to 14 August 2026 and comprises twelve
stakeholder workshops, a documentation review and a technical assessment of the
incumbent origination platform. Build commences 1 September 2026.

## 6. Constraints acknowledged at kick-off

The platform must be delivered on Meridian's existing private cloud tenancy. The
programme must not require a change to the core banking platform's published
integration contract during the initial release.
