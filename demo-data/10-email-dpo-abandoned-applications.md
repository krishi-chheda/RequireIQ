Priya, Tom,

Following up on the action from Workshop 03. Legal have now confirmed the
position on applications that do not proceed to an account being opened.

Personal data captured in abandoned applications must be deleted within 30 days
of the application being marked abandoned. An application is marked abandoned if
the applicant takes no action for 30 consecutive days, so the practical maximum
lifetime of that data is 60 days from last activity.

To be clear about why: where no account is opened, no customer relationship
exists, and the Money Laundering Regulations retention obligation does not
attach. Holding that data longer is a data minimisation breach, and we have had a
supervisory conversation about exactly this in another part of the group.

The system must delete all personal data associated with an abandoned
application, including any partially uploaded identity document images.

Two things I want to flag before you write this into the specification.

First, I am aware the retention schedule MB-DG-011 says seven years. That clause
is written for customers. It does not read cleanly for non-customers and I am
raising a change to section 4 of the schedule. Until that change lands, the two
documents will appear to disagree, and I would rather the programme design to
this email than to the schedule.

Second, if screening has already produced a positive sanctions match on an
abandoned application, that is a different case and the record must be retained
under the financial crime standard. Sofia, please confirm you agree.
