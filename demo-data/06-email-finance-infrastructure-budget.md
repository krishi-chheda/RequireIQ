Following the capital committee on Wednesday, I can now give you the envelope
you asked for in week one.

Infrastructure spend for the onboarding platform must not exceed $200,000 in the
first twelve months of operation. That figure covers compute, storage, network
and the private cloud tenancy uplift. It does not cover the screening vendor
licence, which sits in the financial crime cost centre, and it does not cover
Northgate's fees.

Any spend above that threshold requires a separate capital request, which in
practice means the November committee at the earliest and a three to four month
delay. I would strongly prefer we design inside the envelope rather than plan for
an uplift we may not get.

Two further points the committee asked me to pass on:

The programme must deliver measurable benefit within the first financial year.
The committee's patience for multi-year payback on digital projects is thin at
the moment.

Licence and vendor commitments must be structured so they can be exited within
twelve months. We have been burned on a five-year contract before.

I am copying Kenji so engineering has the number early. Kenji, if the
architecture you are designing cannot live inside $200k please tell me now and
not in December.
