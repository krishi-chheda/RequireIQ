# MB-DG-011 Records Retention Schedule - Retail Banking (extract)

Version 7.2. Owner: Data Governance. Approved by the Group Records Committee.

## 3. Customer identity and onboarding records

### 3.1 Identity verification records

Customer identity records must be retained for seven years from the date of
account closure. This period is set by the Money Laundering Regulations and is
not discretionary.

Identity records in scope of this clause include the verified name, date of
birth, residential address history, the identity document reference and the
outcome of each verification check.

### 3.2 Screening outcomes

Sanctions, politically exposed person and adverse media screening outcomes must
be retained for seven years from the date of the screening event.

### 3.3 Decision records

Account opening decision records, including the rationale for any manual
override, must be retained for seven years from the date of the decision.

### 3.4 Raw document images

Images of identity documents captured during onboarding are held under a separate
disposal rule which is currently under review by the Data Protection Office. The
prevailing instruction is that images must be disposed of once verification is
complete and the verification outcome has been recorded.

## 4. Applications not proceeding to account opening

This section is currently under review. Refer queries to the Data Protection
Officer.

## 5. Legal hold

Where a record is subject to a legal hold, the retention periods in section 3 are
suspended and the record must not be disposed of until the hold is lifted.
