Daniel,

I need to raise a sequencing problem rather than object to the date itself.

Regulatory sign-off for a new onboarding channel requires a 12-week observation
period, which must commence after user acceptance testing completes and must run
on production-equivalent data. That is the standard the Financial Crime Committee
applied to the digital savings launch and I have no basis to ask for a shorter
one here.

If UAT completes at the end of January, twelve weeks of observation takes us into
late April. I do not see how 2 March works unless UAT completes in early
December, which would mean build finishing in about ten weeks.

I am not trying to be difficult. I would rather we have this conversation in
August than in February.

Sofia
