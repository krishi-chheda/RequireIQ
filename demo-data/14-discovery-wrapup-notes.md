# Discovery Wrap-up - Open Items Sweep

Date: 14 August 2026. Open floor session. Attendance not formally recorded.

The purpose of the session was to sweep up anything raised during discovery that
had not landed in a workshop. Items below were called out from the floor. Where
the notes do not name a requester, the transcription did not capture who was
speaking.

## Items raised

- The system must provide a manual override for relationship managers so a
  priority customer can be progressed ahead of the queue.

- The system must support a paper application path for customers who cannot
  complete the journey digitally.

- The platform must integrate with the branch appointment booking system so a
  failed digital verification can be converted into a branch appointment in one
  step.

- The system should retain the applicant's uploaded documents so they do not
  have to be re-uploaded if the application is reopened.

- The system must produce a monthly management information pack covering
  application volume, conversion, abandonment stage and average time to
  activation.

- Somebody asked whether we are handling joint applications. No decision taken.
  Carried as an open question.

## Facilitator note

Several of these items arrived without an identified owner. Priya flagged in the
room that items without a named business owner cannot be taken into build,
because there is nobody to sign off the acceptance criteria. The manual override
item in particular was raised twice in discovery and has still not been claimed by
a named individual.

## Next steps

Requirements register to be finalised and issued for sign-off by 21 August 2026.
