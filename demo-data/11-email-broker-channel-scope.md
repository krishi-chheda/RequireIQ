Subject: Broker channel - needs to be in release 1

Daniel, Priya,

I know discovery is nearly closed but this has come from the growth review on
Tuesday and it is not optional from a commercial standpoint.

Thirty-one per cent of our new current account volume last year came through
introducer relationships. If the new platform only serves direct applicants we
will be running two origination systems for another eighteen months, and the
broker experience stays on the 2014 stack.

So: the onboarding platform must also support broker-introduced applications in
release 1.

Practically that means a few things.

The system must allow an authorised broker to submit an application on behalf of
a customer. The system must provide a partner dashboard where a broker can see
the status of every application they have introduced. The system must calculate
and record introducer commission against each activated account.

I appreciate the baseline was signed on 24 July and this lands after it. I am
happy to take it through change control, but I want it understood that the
commercial case for the programme was built assuming broker volume, so treating
it as a phase 2 item undermines the benefit numbers Helena is holding us to.

Can we discuss on Monday.

Nathan
